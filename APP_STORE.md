## English

### Subtitle
Expansion vessel calculator

### Promotional text
Quickly estimate expansion vessel size, pre-charge and fill pressure directly on iPhone and iPad.

### Description
MAGCalc is a compact calculation aid for heating, HVAC and service technicians.

Features:
- Size heating expansion vessels for water-based systems
- Estimate water expansion from system volume and temperature
- Convert static height into pressure
- Calculate suggested pre-charge and fill pressure
- Show the next common vessel size
- German and English
- Works offline

MAGCalc Pro permanently unlocks manual expansion sizing for glycol, solar fluids and special media with a one-time In-App Purchase.

Important:
MAGCalc is a calculation aid for trained professionals. Manufacturer data, applicable standards, safety components, system design and actual measurements always take precedence.

## App privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no Kamilunavo backend for calculation data. Inputs are processed locally on the device. StoreKit is used for the optional one-time Pro purchase.

## Review notes

MAGCalc is an offline calculation utility for trained heating/HVAC professionals. No account or login is required. The heating vessel calculator and pressure assistant are available immediately.

MAGCalc Pro is a one-time non-consumable In-App Purchase that unlocks the advanced manual-expansion calculator for glycol, solar fluids and special media.

Product ID: `de.kamilunavo.magcalc.pro`.

The advanced calculator requires the user to enter the fluid's volumetric expansion based on manufacturer data; MAGCalc does not claim to calculate solar stagnation or collector vapour volumes automatically.
