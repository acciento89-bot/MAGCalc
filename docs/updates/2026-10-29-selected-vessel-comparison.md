# Documented vessel-capacity comparison — 29 October 2026

A saved water scenario can now document a selected or existing vessel by positive finite nominal liters, manufacturer/model designation and traceable source. The editor preview, scenario list and DE/EN calculation report compare that documented nominal volume against this scenario's computed requirement: selected minus required, and any numerical shortfall. Labels explicitly say “this calculated value”; a nonnegative margin is not a manufacturer or safety approval. No additional manufacturer limits or catalogue rules are invented.

Existing water-expansion, pressure, reserve and recommended-size calculations are unchanged. Selection is optional: old records receive no invented vessel. Scenario duplication preserves a value copy which can be edited independently. Bundle, Pro purchase and advanced-calculation gate, archive path and baseline identities remain intact. The reader retains bare-array/schema1 support; new schema2 writes protect selection metadata from old strict readers. Invalid documented selections and corrupt/future files cannot overwrite existing data.

A behavioral retention regression failed against the old decoder before implementation. The full original and extended pure Swift suite now passes, including positive/nonfinite/source validation, a known20l selection shortfall, equal/above boundaries, independent scenario edits, source/scope report, legacy reads, cold reload and corrupted selection protection. Changed SwiftUI syntax parses and DE/EN strings lint. Full iOS UI typechecking/build and runtime/device QA remain pending; no store, signing or real transactions were performed.
